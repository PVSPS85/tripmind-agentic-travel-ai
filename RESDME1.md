# TripMind AI

## Intelligent Agentic AI Travel Planner for Mixed-Age Group Travel

---

# Project Overview

TripMind AI is a premium AI-powered travel planning platform designed to help users create highly personalized trips for mixed-age groups such as families, friends, seniors, and children.

The platform uses Agentic AI workflows to intelligently generate:

* personalized itineraries
* hotel recommendations
* food recommendations
* activity suggestions
* transport planning
* weather-aware schedules
* budget-aware optimization
* smart route flow

The system focuses on solving one major real-world problem:

> Planning trips for groups with different age categories, comfort levels, interests, budgets, and travel preferences is difficult and time-consuming.

TripMind AI solves this problem using:

* AI agents
* CrewAI orchestration
* multi-LLM architecture
* adaptive recommendation systems
* explainable AI logic

---

# Problem Statement

Travel planning becomes complicated when:

* groups contain children, adults, and seniors
* budgets are limited
* weather conditions change
* people have different food preferences
* travel comfort varies
* users do not know local attractions

Most current travel platforms only provide:

* hotel booking
* static recommendations
* generic itineraries

They do NOT intelligently optimize travel for:

* mixed age groups
* energy levels
* walking effort
* weather safety
* comfort
* dynamic budget adaptation

TripMind AI solves this using intelligent AI-based adaptive planning.

3. commits changes
